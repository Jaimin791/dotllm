## Brand Guidelines for Designers

- **Primary Color:** Black (#000000)
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF) or Light Grey (#F5F5F5)
- **Accent:** Green (#27C93F) for success states only
- **Font:** Inter for headings, JetBrains Mono for code
- **Style:** Minimal, clean, professional
- **Avoid:** Bright colors, gradients, busy backgrounds, decorative elements
